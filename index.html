Common questions

Will applying affect my credit score?

No. Protecting your credit score is our priority. We compare every lender and option on our panel to find you the best solution from the outset, so we get it right the first time and avoid the unnecessary enquiries that can make approval harder down the track. The only time your credit file is touched is when you give us the green light to submit your application.

How long does the process take?

We can often secure a pre-approval within 24 hours, so you don't miss out on the car you're after and have your funds ready when you need them. Timelines vary by lender and your individual profile, but we work nights and weekends to keep things moving and get you sorted as fast as possible.

Which lenders do you work with?

We work with an extensive panel of over 50 specialised lenders, so we have a solution for almost every situation. Our relationships with those lenders often give us access to sharper rates than you'd get going direct. We compare your options right across the panel to find the best rate for your profile, which is frequently lower than the banks.

What kind of employment do I need to apply?

We work with a wide range of lenders, so we can assist if you're employed full-time, part-time, or casually. We also support business applications, including sole traders, companies, and trusts.

What documents will I need?

The documents we need can vary depending on your application and the lender we go to. In most cases, you'll need proof of identity, proof of income, and details of any existing liabilities. We'll guide you through the exact requirements based on your circumstances and the loan type.

What can I finance?

Almost anything, structured as either a secured or unsecured loan. That covers cars, utes, caravans, boats, bikes and personal loans. If it's not on the list, just ask.
