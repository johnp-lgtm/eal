Common questions

Will applying affect my credit score?

Making an enquiry with us won't affect your credit score. We're careful with your credit file at every stage and will always talk you through anything before it happens.

I'm on a casual contract or a visa — can you still help?

Absolutely. Casual employment, visa holders, self-employed, new to the country — these are situations we deal with all the time and know how to navigate.

My credit isn't perfect. Is it worth applying?

Yes. We can handle just about every situation there is, including low or impaired credit. Have a chat with us and we'll give you an honest read on your options.

How fast can I be approved?

We move quickly. Many customers receive an answer the same day. The sooner we have your details, the sooner we can get to work.

What can I finance?

Cars are our bread and butter, but we also arrange finance for utes, caravans, boats, bikes and personal lending. If you're not sure, just ask.
