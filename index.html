Common questions

What is pre-approval and why does it matter?

Pre-approval is a quick assessment of your financial situation that gives you a clear idea of how much you can borrow and at what rate. It helps you shop with confidence and moves the formal approval process much faster.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Our job is to protect your credit score. Making lots of enquiries for finance will impact your credit score, making it harder to be approved and worsening the results you qualify for. As a broker, our job is to compare all the lenders upfront and find you the best result, protecting your credit score and making sure you're matched with the best lender for your situation.

How long does the process take?

Most clients receive a pre-approval outcome within 24 hours. Full settlement timelines vary by loan type, but our team works to make the process as fast as possible.

What kind of employment do I need to apply?

We work with a wide range of lenders, so we can assist if you're employed full-time, part-time, or casually. We also support business applications, including sole traders, companies, and trusts.

What documents will I need to apply?

In most cases, you'll need proof of identity (driver's licence or passport), proof of income, and details of any existing liabilities. We'll guide you through the exact requirements based on your circumstances and the loan type.

My credit isn't perfect. Is it worth applying?

Yes. We can handle just about every situation there is, including low or impaired credit. Have a chat with us and we'll give you an honest read on your options.

What can I finance?

Cars are our bread and butter, but we also arrange finance for utes, caravans, boats, bikes and personal lending. If you're not sure, just ask.
